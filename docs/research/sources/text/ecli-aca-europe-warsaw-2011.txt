Lacking a Unique Identifier

 How to cite?

     European Court of Justice, 14-10-2004, C-
         299/02, European Court reports 2004, Page I-
         09761, Celex-nr 62002J0299,
         CASELEX:EU:2004:8, BAILII: [2004] EUECJ C-
         299/02, VLEX-17284540, NJ 2005, 421, a.s.o.

 How to search?

     Trial and error

 How to hyperlink?

     Nearly impossible to hyperlink to a decision, if
         not published at a specific location.

Lacking a Unique Identifier

 The case number

      is not unique (within or between courts)
      has no strict format
      contains / ( ) [ ] - or other characters that also occur as

          search engine instructions

 The name of the court

      can be abbreviated or translated

 The date of the judgment

      has no strict format

 Other identifiers are often vendor and/or medium
    specific.
