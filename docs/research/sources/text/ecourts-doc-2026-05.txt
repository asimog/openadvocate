Sub.: Automated Case Information Retrieval and Entry in ICMIS

1. To further streamline case information management, administrative efficiency and ensure
    better administration of justice by minimizing errors likely to occur in manual data entry, the
    Court's case management software [ICMIS] is now upgraded to automatically fetch the
    relevant case-related data from the database of High Courts, District Courts [including FIR and
    Police Station related information from the official data maintained by Government]. Section
    I-B shall henceforth enter the case information in ICMIS, as may be automatically fetched by
    virtue of such mechanism.

2. Learned AdvocatesonRecord and PartiesinPerson are encouraged to henceforth mention in
    the Proforma for First Listing, the CNR (Case Number Record)1 of the matter as was assigned
    to the matter by the concerned Court whose order/judgment is under challenge. This would
    facilitate retrieval of case details from the concerned Court in automated manner, thereby
    ensuring accuracy of data and efficient case management.

3. CNR number of a particular case can be retrieved using the eCourts official website, through
    the Case Status option, available for District Courts2 and High Courts3 by searching the case
    using the relevant party name, case number, filing number, or other available parameters.
