new case categories is enclosed (Annexure - B).

to the Supreme Court Rules, 2013 (As Amended), is also enclosed (Annexure - C).

new case categories in the e-filing module and listing proforma with due care.

matter would be categorized as 1502.
In Criminal matters, if there are 2 special offences invoked. For example, POCSO
Act or SC/ST are invoked, the cases fall under the following categories:
• Wherever the offence against women, it would fall under 1501.
• There is an offence against child [including girl child], the same would
come under 1502.
• Wherever NDPS Act is invoked, it would come under 1504.
• Wherever Money Laundering is invoked, it would fall under 1509.
