   FOUNDATION OF THE CASE
   INFORMATION SYSTEM

 It was initiated by the eCommittee,
 Supreme Court of India.

 Developed by National Informatics
 Centre (NIC).

 Under eCourts Mission Mode Project
 (Phase I-III).

 By the Department of Justice,
 Government of India.
               FIRST DEPLOYMENT

 The NIC began building the first version of
 CIS (CIS 1.0) around 2006-2007.

 It was designed as a standalone desktop-
 based application, deployed on a per-court
 basis (not networked or web-enabled).

 CIS 1.0 piloted in selected District and
 Sessions Courts across states like Punjab,
 Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Maharashtra in
 the year 2007-2008.

 MAIN PURPOSES AND GOALS OF
THE CASE INFORMATION SYSTEM

                         (CIS)

 Digitization of Judicial Processes.

 Enhancing Transparency.

 Improving Judicial Efficiency.

 Monitoring and Performance Analytics.

 Integration Across the Justice
  Ecosystem.
  MAIN PURPOSES AND GOALS OF
THE CASE INFORMATION SYSTEM

                          (CIS)

 Empowering Litigants and Lawyers.

 Standardization of Judicial Data.

 Promoting E-Governance in Judiciary.

 Speedy Disposal of Cases.

 Facilitate Policy Implementation and
 Reforms.
    DIGITIZATION OF JUDICIAL
    PROCESSES

 Replace manual registers with digital
 databases.

 Ensure electronic filing, tracking, and
 disposal of cases.

 Support the transition toward paperless
 courts.
    ENHANCING TRANSPARENCY

 Make court proceedings, case status,
 and judgments publicly accessible.

 Enable real-time updates for litigants,
 lawyers, and the public.

 Reduce discretionary power through
 standardized workflows.
 IMPROVING JUDICIAL EFFICIENCY

 Automate daily tasks: case listing,
 scheduling, cause list generation, etc.

 Support quicker case movement through
 stage-wise tracking.

 Minimize manual errors and procedural
 delays.
 MONITORING & PERFORMANCE
 ANALYTICS

Provide dashboards for judges and
administrative heads to monitor:

  Case pendency
  Disposal rate
  Court performance

 ECOSYSTEM/STAKEHOLDERS

  EMPOWERING LITIGANTS &
   LAWYERS

Offer 24x7 access to:

 Case status through metadata.
 To obtain Orders/judgments online.
 Court calendars.
  STANDARDIZATION OF JUDICIAL
   DATA

 To create uniform formats and case types
 across all district and subordinate courts.

 Ensure consistent reporting and tracking for
 High Courts and Supreme Court oversight.
  PROMOTING E-GOVERNANCE IN
   JUDICIARY

 E-Filing
 E-Payment
 Virtual hearings
 Certified copies online (Copying Branch)
It supports transparent and accountable

 governance.
  SPEEDY DISPOSAL OF CASES

 Enable better judicial time management
 and tracking of long-pending matters.

 Prioritize urgent cases through automated
 case filtering and tagging.
 FACILITATE POLICY
  IMPLEMENTATION & REFORMS

It provides central data through NJDG
for:

 Government schemes.

 Law Commission studies.

 Supreme Court and High Court
  monitoring.
                    SUMMARY

The Case Information System aims to:
 Digitize,
 Automate,
 and Unify the functioning of trial courts to

 Ensure fast, transparent, and citizen-
 friendly delivery of justice.
