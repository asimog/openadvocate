         About eCourts                                                                                                           eCourts

The eCourts, a Mission Mode Project, was
conceptualized by "National Policy and Action
Plan for Implementation of Information and
Communication Technology (ICT) in the Indian
Judiciary - 2005", submitted by e-Committee
(Supreme Court of India), with a vision to
transform the Indian judiciary by ICT
enablement of courts.
